Tap Away Arrows is an offline puzzle game. You can play without creating an account.

## Information stored on your device

The app saves your unlocked levels, earned stars, onboarding status and settings (sound, music and vibration) on your device. This information is used to restore your progress and preferences. It is not sent to the publisher. You can reset game progress in Settings. You can remove all locally stored app data through your device settings or by uninstalling the app.

## Information sent from the app

The current release has no account, advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, social-sharing or cloud-sync service. The splash video, onboarding animation, levels, graphics and sounds are bundled with the app. The app does not intentionally send game progress or personal information to a server. The onboarding animation was sourced from an external CDN during development and is included in the installed app; it is not downloaded while you play.

## Device access

The app uses device vibration for optional feedback and audio output for game sounds. It does not request location, camera, contacts or microphone access. You can turn sound and vibration preferences off in Settings.

## Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from children. A parent or guardian may contact us at [SUPPORT EMAIL] with a privacy question. The publisher must confirm the intended audience and complete the corresponding Google Play declarations before publication.

## Security and retention

Game progress remains on your device until you reset it, clear app data or uninstall the app. Because it is stored locally, the publisher cannot retrieve or delete a copy of that progress from a server.

## Changes and contact

If the app gains online features or its data practices change, this policy will be updated before those features are released. For privacy questions, contact [SUPPORT EMAIL].
